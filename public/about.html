About the cinema

About 347movies

347movies is a free movie website: watch full films in your browser with zero cost, zero accounts, and zero ads interrupting the movie. It streams public domain and Creative Commons films embedded from the Internet Archive — so storage and bandwidth for video cost us nothing, and the films stay free forever.

Legal-only, always

Every film on this site must carry a declared public domain or Creative Commons license, verified from archive.org's own license metadata before it appears. Films whose legal status cannot be verified are excluded, never included on a guess. We never embed, link to, or scrape pirated streams, unauthorized mirrors, or torrent sources.

How the site works

Films play in an embedded player served by the Internet Archive (archive.org/embed/<identifier>). We never host, store, or proxy video bytes — the archive does, and their player and privacy practices apply when you press play. Search and browse are powered by our own API, which queries archive.org's public catalog and caches normalized results for 24 hours.

How we make money (transparently)

347movies is free to viewers, and we intend to keep it that way. Two honest revenue streams:

  • Display ads in clearly labeled sidebar and leaderboard slots. Ads never appear over, inside, before, or after the player — the movie is sacred. Ad slots are currently reserved; no ad network is rendering right now.
  • Affiliate links (Amazon Associates-style) on films that are not freely watchable. These are always visibly disclosed, open with rel="sponsored noopener", and never replace a free watch. Because every catalog film is freely watchable, no affiliate link is currently shown on any film.

If an ad placement would ever interrupt a film, the answer is no. We would rather earn less than break the viewing experience.
